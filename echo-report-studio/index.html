    Where & how to measure

    Salur utama selepas pulmonary valve, sebelum bercabang menjadi RPA dan LPA.

    Right pulmonary artery (RPA)

    Where & how to measure

    Cabang pulmonary artery ke paru-paru kanan. Rekod hanya jika sempadan salur dapat dikenal pasti.

    PA bifurcation view, ukur bahagian proximal cabang pada end-diastole.

    Contoh ayat: Right pulmonary artery 1.7 cm.

    Belum diukur. Ruangan kosong tidak dianggap normal.

    Left pulmonary artery (LPA)

    Where & how to measure

    Cabang pulmonary artery ke paru-paru kiri. Jangan guna cut-off MPA untuk cabang ini.

    PA bifurcation view, ukur bahagian proximal cabang pada end-diastole.

    Contoh ayat: Left pulmonary artery 1.6 cm.

    Belum diukur. Ruangan kosong tidak dianggap normal.

    ASE 2025 Table 1 (adult MPA): <2.5 cm normal; 2.5–3.0 mild; >3.0–3.5 moderate; >3.5 severe enlargement. Saiz PA sahaja tidak menentukan pulmonary hypertension. Cut-off ini tidak digunakan untuk RPA / LPA atau kanak-kanak.

    Reference note · 2.5 cm boundary

    Table 1 meletakkan 2.5 cm dalam mild enlargement; teks guideline menyatakan >25 mm abnormal. Auto category menggunakan Table 1 secara konsisten. Semak nilai sempadan dalam konteks klinikal.

    Reporting rules & calculation notes

    Based on the supplied reporting prompt. This is a structured template and calculator, not an AI interpreter or a validated diagnostic system. The general adult sinus-rhythm diastolic pathway is automatically assessed using ASE 2025; excluded populations need a separate clinical assessment.

    Direct BSA takes priority; otherwise Du Bois BSA is used. Input units can be changed using compatible unit selectors. Calculations and standard report measurements retain their clinical units; mass sizes retain the selected cm/mm unit. Direct measured LAVI takes priority when entered; otherwise LAVI uses the supplied measured LA volume; separate single-plane volumes are not averaged into a biplane volume.

    Average E/e′ = mitral E / [(septal e′ + lateral e′) / 2], per ASE 2025 Table 3. Figures 2 and 3 inform the automated resting diastolic assessment; this is not a diagnosis of HFpEF.

    WMSI uses assessable segments 1–16 only; apical cap is excluded. Unspecified segments remain unassessed unless you explicitly mark them normal. Conventional LV geometry requires sex and BSA and may be unreliable in asymmetric hypertrophy.

    Reference framework: ASE/EACVI chamber quantification (2015); ASE diastolic function and right-heart assessment updates (2025). Verify clinical interpretation against the applicable full guideline at the ASE guidelines library.

    Report format: ASE adult echo reporting (2025). Valve severity is selected after integrated assessment; colour jet appearance alone does not determine severity. Normal presets are operator confirmations.

    Automatic grades: ASE/EACVI chamber quantification, Supplemental Table 3 (2015); ASE right heart, Table 1 (2025). Continuous values use the upper normal/mild/moderate limits; shared TAPSE 1.3 cm and S′ 7.2 cm/s endpoints are labelled borderline. Pressure grades describe the estimated pressure, not a diagnosis of pulmonary hypertension. Valve grading requires integrated valve measurements.

    Description terminology: ASE adult reporting (2025), Table 9. Mass assessment: ASE cardiac sources of embolism (2016). Brief explanations and examples are teaching aids and are not included in patient reports.
