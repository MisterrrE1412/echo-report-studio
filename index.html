Reporting rules & calculation notes

Based on the supplied reporting prompt. This is a structured template and calculator, not an AI interpreter or a validated diagnostic system. The general adult sinus-rhythm diastolic pathway is automatically assessed using ASE 2025; excluded populations need a separate clinical assessment.

Direct BSA takes priority; otherwise Du Bois BSA is used. Input units can be changed using compatible unit selectors. Calculations and standard report measurements retain their clinical units; mass sizes retain the selected cm/mm unit. Direct measured LAVI takes priority when entered; otherwise LAVI uses the supplied measured LA volume; separate single-plane volumes are not averaged into a biplane volume.

Average E/e′ = mitral E / [(septal e′ + lateral e′) / 2], per ASE 2025 Table 3. Figures 2 and 3 inform the automated resting diastolic assessment; this is not a diagnosis of HFpEF.

WMSI uses assessable segments 1–16 only; apical cap is excluded. Unspecified segments remain unassessed unless you explicitly mark them normal. Conventional LV geometry requires sex and BSA and may be unreliable in asymmetric hypertrophy.

Reference framework: ASE/EACVI chamber quantification (2015); ASE diastolic function and right-heart assessment updates (2025). Verify clinical interpretation against the applicable full guideline at the ASE guidelines library.
