Reporting rules & calculation notes

Based on the supplied reporting prompt. This is a structured template and calculator, not an AI interpreter or a validated diagnostic system. The general adult sinus-rhythm diastolic pathway is automatically assessed using ASE 2025; excluded populations need a separate clinical assessment.

Direct BSA takes priority; otherwise Du Bois BSA is used. Input units can be changed using compatible unit selectors. Calculations and standard report measurements retain their clinical units; mass sizes retain the selected cm/mm unit. Direct measured LAVI takes priority when entered; otherwise LAVI uses the supplied measured LA volume; separate single-plane volumes are not averaged into a biplane volume.

Average E/e′ = mitral E / [(septal e′ + lateral e′) / 2], per ASE 2025 Table 3. Figures 2 and 3 inform the automated resting diastolic assessment; this is not a diagnosis of HFpEF.

WMSI uses assessable segments 1–16 only; apical cap is excluded. Unspecified segments remain unassessed unless you explicitly mark them normal. Conventional LV geometry requires sex and BSA and may be unreliable in asymmetric hypertrophy.

Reference framework: ASE/EACVI chamber quantification (2015); ASE diastolic function and right-heart assessment updates (2025). Verify clinical interpretation against the applicable full guideline at the ASE guidelines library.

VERSION HISTORY

What’s new

Changes and improvements in each release. Latest version: v26.

v26 · Latest2026-10-31

Keep diastolic measurement inputs focused

  • Fixed diastolic unit inputs losing keyboard focus after the first digit. Hidden canonical measurement fields no longer disable their visible input boxes.
  • Focused measurement text is preserved during automatic assessment updates, including multi-digit and decimal entry. Formatting is applied after leaving the field or explicitly changing its unit.
  • A measurement row remains open while it is being edited, even when an automatic Yes / No category changes. Special-population eligibility and intentional field exclusions remain active.
v252026-10-30

Flexible measurement units and faster loading

  • Added compatible unit selectors for dimensions, velocities, times, pressures, areas, volumes, indexed volumes, height, weight and BSA. Ratios and percentages retain their appropriate units.
  • Switching units converts the displayed value while keeping calculations and stored measurements in their standard clinical units. Saved studies and recovery retain unit preferences; older backups remain compatible.
  • Vegetation, thrombus and mass dimensions support linked cm/mm choices, including the selected unit in report wording.
  • Reduced startup script requests, deferred script loading and optimized the transparent logo. Echo-view wall-motion drawings are created only when needed, and unchanged scores avoid repeated repainting.
  • Coalesced draft saving during rapid typing while retaining immediate saves on changes, page exit and backgrounding.
  • Translated the full What’s new page and previous release entries into English.
v242026-10-22

Great arteries: aorta and pulmonary artery

  • Added a Great arteries section for the aortic root, proximal ascending aorta, annulus, STJ, and main, right and left pulmonary arteries.
  • Added acquisition views, measurement timing, location guidance and sample wording for junior CVTs. Optional measurements remain collapsed.
  • Adult main pulmonary artery size categories follow ASE 2025 Table 1. Aortic size interpretation remains operator-selected, with diameter/BSA indexing when BSA is available.
  • Measurements and interpretation are included in the report and conclusion. Not measurable and unreliable values are excluded from valid measurements.
  • New fields participate in saved studies, backups and draft recovery.
  • Added a record-ID fallback so draft recovery can initialize in browsers without randomUUID.
v232026-10-20

Diastolic measurements linked correctly

  • Reliable numeric measurements now populate unselected automatic diastolic choices. Explicit manual choices and N/A selections are preserved.
  • E/e′ and E/A can use the available original measurements. Inflow-derived E/A requires separable E and A waves; automatic LAVI and PV S/D use requires confirmation of reliability and applicability.
  • The normal tricuspid valve preset no longer clears TR Vmax, TR max gradient or measurable-TR status. Hemodynamic measurements are preserved.
  • A newly entered TR measurement updates signal status. Older records containing both no measurable TR and a TR value require confirmation before that value is used.
  • The entry review identifies measurements used, unconfirmed or excluded. Stored reports requiring recalculation are flagged without deleting edits.
  • Incomplete assessments now give more specific reasons. Unavailable TR alone does not establish normal function or a diastolic grade.
v222026-10-20

Clearer choices for junior CVTs

  • No measurable TR is linked to the diastolic N/A choice. Sufficient alternative parameters can support LAP assessment and grading; absent TR is not treated as normal.
  • Reorganized the valve interface to show one valve at a time. Regurgitation and stenosis have distinct labels and colors, with supporting measurements available on demand.
  • Added structured LV thrombus and spontaneous echo contrast assessment within the LV section, including location, size, shape, texture, mobility and attachment.
  • Added short term explanations and sample sentences for mass, valve morphology and CW Doppler choices. Teaching notes are excluded from patient reports.
  • Added this What’s new page to record version changes, without additional large images or runtime libraries.
v212026-10-19

Quick selection, masses and valve details

  • Removed the diastolic guided workflow. Quick selection retains special-population screening and reveals numeric fields under the relevant choices.
  • Pulmonary pressure calculation uses TR max gradient plus RAP for estimated RVSP, with PASP when outflow obstruction is absent.
  • Added structured vegetation, thrombus and mass descriptions, plus supporting measurements for valve regurgitation and stenosis.
  • Improved legacy-record restoration and checks for conflicts between numeric values and selected categories.
v202026-10-11

Plan acquisition from the study indication

  • Added Plan this study to suggest acquisition steps, views, measurements and Doppler modes for indications such as PE, AS and MR.
  • Added acquisition checklists, PW/CW explanations, lightweight diagrams and source links.
  • Added a discussion-prompt copy option for ChatGPT without automatically sending patient details.

Release history is recorded from version 20. Term explanations are teaching aids; clinical reports require operator review.
